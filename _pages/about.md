I am a faculty member at the Department of Astronomy, Shanghai Jiao Tong University. Before this, I did my post-doc research at Caltech/IPAC, working with Dr. [Yun Wang](https://web.ipac.caltech.edu/staff/wang/) and University of Waterloo, working with Prof. [Will Percival](https://uwaterloo.ca/physics-astronomy/profile/wperciva). Before moving to the west coast, I obtained my PhD from the Department of Physics at New York University, under the supervision of Prof. [Michael Blanton](https://cosmo.nyu.edu/mb144/) and Prof. [Jeremy Tinker](https://cosmo.nyu.edu/~tinker/). 

I am interested in many aspects of cosmology and astrophysics, including but not limited to large scale structure, galaxy clustering, dark matter & dark energy, early universe, galaxy formation and evolution. My research works can be found from the Research page.
